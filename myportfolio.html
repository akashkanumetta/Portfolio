05 — Intellectual Property

Patent filed

⬡ Filed · February 2025
Process for Enhancement of Permanently Shadowed Regions in Lunar Crater Images Using Multi-Algorithmic Image Processing
A novel multi-algorithm pipeline to recover detail from the permanently shadowed regions of lunar craters — areas that receive no direct sunlight and are invisible to conventional imaging. The method fuses Multi-Scale Retinex, Dark Channel Prior, CLAHE, and Unsharp Masking in a staged pipeline, achieving significant improvement in surface detail visibility for scientific and navigational applications.
